## Pursue *personal growth*

You are the "lid" to business growth.

**1.** What are you doing to…

- Grow you?
- Grow your people?

## Define your *market* — and how you serve it

**2.** What and why do our customers want to buy from us?

**3.** How can, and do, we give them what they want and expect?

## Understand your *past* and *present*

**4.** Where have we been?

**5.** Where are we now?

## Clarify your *vision*

Your overarching future direction — the dream.

**6.** Where do we want to be?

## Establish your *strategy*

The general approach or plan to achieve your objectives.

**7.** What do we want to accomplish?

## Develop your *tactical* plans

The action steps for implementing your strategies.

**8.** How can we get there?

**9.** When do we want to arrive?

**10.** Who can make it happen on schedule?

## Set your measurable performance *goals*

Activity targets with numbers and timeliness.

**11.** What is our goal?

## Pause and evaluate

Review your strategic and tactical plans, and your measurable goals.

**12.** Are we making progress?
