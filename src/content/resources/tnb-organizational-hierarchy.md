The Values-Driven Organizational Hierarchy shows how everything in an organization connects, from who you are down to how you measure success. **Effective leadership** sits at the centre of it all.

## The hierarchy

**Core Values** — Who are we?

**Purpose** — Why do we exist?

**Vision** — Where are we going?

Those three flow into:

**Super-Objectives** — What do we want to accomplish every day?

**Strategic Plans** — What do we want to accomplish over the long term?

**Tactical Plans** — How can we get there? When do we want to arrive? Who can make it happen on schedule?

**Measurable Goals** — What are our numeric and timely measures of success?

## Working ON the business, not just IN it

The diagram divides the hierarchy in two, and the split is the point:

- **15% of leaders work ON the business while they work IN the business** — the levels from core values down through strategic plans.
- **85% of leaders work only IN the business** — the tactical plans and measurable goals at the bottom.
