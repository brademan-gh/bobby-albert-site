I’m talking about the Communicator Coaching ([www.CommunicatorCoaching.com](http://www.CommunicatorCoaching.com)) mastermind event.  I joined several entrepreneurs who gathered in a Colorado cabin to gain clarity and create tangible next-steps for our businesses.

The group was led by Ken Davis ([www.KenDavis.com](http://www.KenDavis.com)), Joy Groblebe, Brian Scheer and Matt Simpson.  These folks are in the business of guiding and equipping entrepreneurs to take their business to the next level.

In the kick-off session, Ken Davis laid out clear signposts on the path to success.  He challenged us to, “Pick your peak (as we looked at the mountains of Colorado), make the commitment, take the first step, look back to see how far you have come, never lose sight of your goal, and don’t forget to CELEBRATE!”

Every leader can realize the following four benefits from pulling back from the daily grind and participating in a mastermind retreat.

**Clarity of thought** — Getting away from daily demands can help you get a whole new perspective on your life and business.

**Build relationships** — When you hang around a group of folks in a relaxed setting for a couple of days, you establish and strengthen relationships that would otherwise take years of “likes” and “tweets” and “favorites”.

**Gain new perspective** — Times like this allow you to hear the opinions and thoughts of other people.  Many times we are so close to a challenge or opportunity that we can’t see it clearly.  Sometimes another person can nail the root issue in a matter of minutes.

**Challenged to act** – This is the fun stuff.  Once you refine and clarify your roadmap, it’s time to act.  Since leaders are “achievers”, when you return from an event like this, it’s time to get going and take the first step toward your newly defined goals.

*How long has it been since you got away to think about your business?  Is it time for you to consider joining a similar group so that you can accelerate the growth of your organization?*
