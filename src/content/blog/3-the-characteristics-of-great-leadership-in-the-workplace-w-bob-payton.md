What makes a good leader?

A delegator?

An inspirer?

A strong force on the front lines?

It can be argued that a good leader consists of all of those things.

But how should leadership in the workplace be encouraged and developed?

In this episode Bob Payton, President and CEO of [Dental Planet](https://www.dentalplanet.com/), gives insight into what good leadership in the workplace looks like. Bob has handled a lot of turnaround management and is all about understanding the customers needs and fulfilling them. His knowledge is invaluable.
