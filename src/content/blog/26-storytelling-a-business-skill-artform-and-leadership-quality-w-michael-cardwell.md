Storytelling.

It’s a business skill, an artform, and a leadership quality. If you are struggling to shift the energy, change the focus, or impact your employees or your customers, maybe you should take a good look at your storytelling skills: They may be the hidden key to unlock your organization’s success.

This week’s guest has won multiple Emmy awards for his unique storytelling, or, as he would want it said: His team has multiple Emmy awards for *their* storytelling. [Michael Cardwell](https://www.linkedin.com/in/micardwell) joined us on this episode of the [Lead to Grow podcast](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/lead-to-grow/id1439112048) to share about leadership and storytelling.

*Michael is the Founder & Creative Director of* [*Digital Brew*](https://www.digitalbrew.com/)*, a visual storytelling agency that creates animated and live-action video content for their clients. Since 2011, they’ve won 16 Emmy awards, eight Tellys, and two Daveys.*
