What initially appears as failure, can often be the catalysts to our greatest discoveries. [Josh Steimle](https://www.linkedin.com/in/joshuasteimle) had an apparent failure — in 2013, he was nearly out of business. A bad hire at his marketing agency had brought him to the brink of bankruptcy.

Fast-forward to the present, and his agency ([MWi](https://www.mwi.com/)) is thriving, he started a second business ([Influencer Inc](https://www.influencerinc.co/).), and he’s now publishing a book called The 7 Systems of Influence, to show others how they can copy his successes (and avoid his failures).

We invited Josh on the [Lead to Grow podcast](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/lead-to-grow/id1439112048?mt=2) to share the premise for his new book, and what he’s learned about leveraging your skillset to influence others.
