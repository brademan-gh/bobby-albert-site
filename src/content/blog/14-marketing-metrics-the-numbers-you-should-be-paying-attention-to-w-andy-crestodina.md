[Andy Crestodina](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andycrestodina){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. He’s a cool guy with a track record that includes earning a degree in Mandarin and working as a teacher in China.

In 2001, Andy and his friend co-founded Orbit Media, a company that has grown from zero to about \$4.5M in revenue. Totally organically.

Now, Andy is the CMO at Orbit Media and he’s a marketing metrics expert.

Our latest guest on the [Lead to Grow](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/lead-to-grow/id1439112048?mt=2){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} podcast, Andy talked with us about his experience and his company. He also shared his metrics expertise, focusing on the metrics you should care about the most. And the ones you should care about the least.
