Customer service is only as good as your employee service. Many believe that, but [Chuck Bailey](https://www.linkedin.com/in/charles-chuck-bailey-793895a) is one who consistently lives it.

He’s the Owner and Founder of [Cornerstone Moving & Storage](https://cornerstonemoving.com/), where they focus on each employee, by getting to know them personally. From day one, Chuck dives into the lives of new team members and dedicates himself to their growth and development.

After you hear his interview on the [Lead to Grow podcast](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/lead-to-grow/id1439112048?mt=2), you may alter your mindset about the magnitude of impact a moving company can have.
