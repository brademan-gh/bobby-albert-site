Leading millennials, new sales representatives, or other employees in entry-level positions has unique challenges.

[Dan Gottlieb](https://www.linkedin.com/in/danielpgottlieb) has some helpful answers to those challenges, and they all come from a massive amount of experience. Dan is an industry analyst at [TOPO Inc](https://topohq.com/)., specifically for Sales Development, & Account-Based Growth Organizations.

Yes, that’s a bit of a mouthful! Essentially, Dan helps sales development companies utilize world-class strategies and tactics.

He came on the [Lead to Grow podcast](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/lead-to-grow/id1439112048?mt=2) to share some of his best practices on working with entry-level sales development representatives (often called SDRs). (He even gave us the inside scoop on working with millennials!)
