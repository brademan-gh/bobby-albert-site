How do you innovate faster and at the same time be more customer-centric?

We spoke with [Aaron Eden](https://www.linkedin.com/in/aaroneden){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"} is Co-Founder and Chief Operating Officer of [Moves The Needle](https://www.movestheneedle.com/){target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. Moves the Needle “ignites entrepreneurial action within large organizations so their people can be closer to customers, move faster, and act bolder.”

He gave us tips on how innovators like Steve Jobs lead their teams to victory.
