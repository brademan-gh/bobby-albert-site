Failure is sometimes a better teacher than success. But too often, when we run into failure, we don’t see the learning opportunity. We believe the lie that it’s the end.

The world is thankful [Todd Palmer](https://www.linkedin.com/in/toddpalmer1/) didn’t stop after his “failure”:

He joined us on the [Lead to Grow](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/lead-to-grow/id1439112048?mt=2) podcast to share what he’s learned from the brink of bankruptcy about failure and humility as a leader.

Todd is the President of [Diversified Industrial Staffing](http://diversifiedindustrialstaffing.com/), a 6-time member of the Inc. 5000. He’s also the Founder and President of [ExtraordinaryAdvisors.Com](http://extraordinaryadvisors.com/), a keynote speaker, and author of [*The Job Search Process*](https://www.amazon.com/Job-Search-Process-Great-weeks/dp/150601075X).
