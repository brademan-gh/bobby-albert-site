It is finally here – my new book, *Principled Profits – Outward Success Is an Inside Job* officially releases today! Click here to read the press release.

It’s been a long time coming.  Hundreds of hours of effort. A labor of love for sure.

*[Principled Profits](http://principledprofitsbook.com/)* contains what I found to be the essential principles of effective leadership. Do you want to lead your family, team, or entire organization with more intention and impact? Then this book is for you.

**The book has six main sections:**

1.  **Leading & Managing** – The important (and different) roles of leading and managing
2.  **Process & Content** – Learn to embrace the *how* as you pursue the *what*
3.  **Principle vs. Expediency** – Direction trumps speed – every time
4.  **Core Values** – Center your leadership on your core values for extraordinary results
5.  **Workplace Culture** – Focus your leadership on the fundamentals, and the right culture will follow
6.  **Goals & Controls** – Perpetuate your leadership by setting goals and monitoring progress

By employing these cornerstone principles, I was able to grow our small business of five employees to over 150 team members before we sold to a publicly traded company. The information found in this book ***transformed my leadership*** and ***the performance of my organization.***
