“A company is nothing more than a group of people.”

I started listening even closer as [Oded](https://www.linkedin.com/in/odedcarmi) spoke. His words resonated with me, for multiple reasons: We had similar ideas on leadership, plus, we’ve both owned companies in the moving business. (Oded is the President of [DN Van Lines](https://dnvanlines.com/).) Lastly, we both believe this:

*The right person is far more important than the perfect skill set.*

On our [Lead to Grow podcast](https://itunes.apple.com/us/podcast/lead-to-grow/id1439112048?mt=2), Oded talked about expanding your company into other Lines of Business, and gave three reasons to focus on the person, and hire from within your company whenever possible.
