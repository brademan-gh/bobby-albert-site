Most of us know the tremendous impact branding can have upon an organization. When most of hear “branding,” we think colors, websites, logos, and names.

But [Bill Gullan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/williamgullan) says that’s not where branding starts.

Bill’s the president of [Finch Brands](https://www.linkedin.com/company/finch-brands), where they help clients brand and rebrand every day.

Bill says that branding starts well before you name a company or polish a website. It starts internally, with the values driven by your culture.

On this episode of the [Lead to Grow podcast](https://itunes.apple.com/gb/podcast/lead-to-grow/id1439112048?mt=2), Bill lays out his specialized plan of why branding is really an inside-out approach. He also gives listeners an inside peek into The Finch Way — Finch’s own values-driven approach to business.
